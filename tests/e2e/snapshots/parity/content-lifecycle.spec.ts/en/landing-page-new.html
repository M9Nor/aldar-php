What is the government guarantee

| The meaning of government guarantee?

It means that the government is a partner in the project, and that the project is under its supervision, in terms of construction quality, accuracy in deadlines and specifications, and in most cases the land of the project is owned by the state, and the implementing agency is a reliable and strong company..

| Why the government guarantee?

Because the property is safe from any possible consequences in delivery, licensing and bankruptcy, and because you are thus avoiding the possibility of working with unstable or unreliable companies

| Government warranty features?

It combines the advantages of the public sector and the features of the private sector, as the quality in construction, complete reliability, safety of legal procedures,
